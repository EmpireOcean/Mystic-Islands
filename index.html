❓ How to Play

🖥 Desktop

  • WASD — Move
  • Mouse — Look around
  • Space — Jump
  • Left click — Attack
  • Right click (hold, with gun) — Aim
  • Scroll wheel — Zoom camera
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — Switch weapon (fist / sword / gun)
  • E — Open bag
  • Esc / P — Pause

📱 Mobile

  • Joystick (bottom-left) — Move
  • Drag right half of screen — Look around
  • Pinch with 2 fingers — Zoom camera
  • ⤒ Jump / ⚔ Attack — Bottom-right buttons
  • 🎯 Aim (hold + drag) — Precise aim, e.g. gun
  • ⟳ Top button — Toggle landscape mode
  • 🎒 Bag — Open bag

Tip: fight monsters, break wooden chests for gold, and reach the goal island to level up!
